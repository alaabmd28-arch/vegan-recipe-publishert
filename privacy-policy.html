Privacy Policy — Vegan Recipe Publisher

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Vegan Recipe Publisher is a personal automation application used by its owner to publish vegan recipe content to Pinterest. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when the application is used.

1. Information We Access

When connected to Pinterest, the application may access the authenticated Pinterest account's information necessary to publish Pins, including the account identifier and the user's own boards and Pins. The application does not request or access other Pinterest users' private information.

2. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through Pinterest is used only to authenticate the account, identify the owner's boards, and create or manage Pins as requested by the account owner.

3. Data Storage and Sharing

Vegan Recipe Publisher does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for advertising purposes. Pinterest authentication credentials and access tokens are used only for the connected Pinterest account and should be stored securely by the account owner and automation environment.

4. Third-Party Services

The application may interact with Pinterest and other services selected by the owner for content hosting or automation. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms. Pinterest's policies are available at Pinterest Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention

The application retains only information needed to operate the publishing workflow. The account owner can disconnect the Pinterest integration and delete locally stored workflow data at any time.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect authentication credentials and workflow data. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

This application is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the contact information associated with the project.
